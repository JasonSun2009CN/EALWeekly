## What is this publication?

EAL Weekly is a public-facing AI journal that tracks models, tools, research, and trends without turning every issue into a technical manual.

We want readers to understand the most important developments in AI this week without needing prior expertise in every term or stack.

## What do we focus on?

The journal covers four main areas:

- AI news: major product launches, policy moves, partnerships, and releases
- AI explained: translating complex concepts into plain language
- Tutorials: helping readers understand models, agents, reasoning, and tooling
- Research and applications: observing which trends are becoming real-world workflows and products

## Why bilingual?

AI is a global conversation. Many readers want to access the same ideas in Chinese and in English, while also comparing them with original sources.

That is why EAL Weekly keeps a bilingual structure: Chinese lowers the barrier to entry, while English supports direct access to the global conversation.

## What problem are we solving?

The problem is not that there is no AI information. The problem is that the information is often fragmented, fast-moving, and hard to parse.

EAL Weekly aims to highlight the most important developments of the week and explain what they actually mean for readers, rather than simply listing headlines.
